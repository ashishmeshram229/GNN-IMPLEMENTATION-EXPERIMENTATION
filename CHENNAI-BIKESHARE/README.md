# Chennai SmartBike — graph construction and temporal link prediction

Trip-data-only study of the Chennai SmartBike system (Jan 2021 – Aug 2022, 197k rentals).
No POI, OpenStreetMap, Google or transit data is used; the only extra input is the operator's
station sheet, for coordinates.

## Notebooks (run in order)

| Notebook | What it does |
|---|---|
| `01_eda.ipynb` | Seven exploratory plots: hourly profile, loop share, station age, distance decay, Moran's I, usage typologies, IMD seasons |
| `02_trips_to_graph.ipynb` | Builds the adjacency matrix `A` (114×114) and the 20 monthly snapshots `S`, documents the station index, reports graph properties and monthly node/pair dynamics |
| `03_link_prediction_baseline.ipynb` | Next-month link prediction: rolling folds, three baselines, metrics by pair type |

## Task definition (notebook 03)

Predict whether directed pair i→j carries at least one trip in month *t*, given months 1…*t*−1.

- 114 stations, 12,882 non-self directed pairs, all scored (no negative sampling)
- Rolling origin: train months 5…*t*−1, test month *t*, 14 folds (Jul 2021 – Aug 2022)
- All features computed with a strict cutoff at *t*−1
- Metrics: AP (primary), ROC-AUC, Precision@k (k = positives), Recall@500

## Known assumptions

- Fixed universe of 114 stations in every month, although 65 stations first appear after month 1
  (no opening dates available; first-observed month is a proxy, not an opening date).
- Absence of a trip is treated as an inactive pair, which cannot be distinguished from a closed
  station, an empty dock or a data gap.
- Self-loops (66% of all trips) are excluded from the adjacency and kept as station features.
- 22 of 114 stations have no coordinates; distance is median-imputed for those pairs.
